# Field validation worksheet

Status: **not yet conducted**. The Open House example and packing-support trial use synthetic parameters. This worksheet is a collection plan, not evidence of real-world effectiveness.

Choose one campus event and record the following with its organizer. Use anonymous participant IDs and aggregate arrival counts; names and contact details are unnecessary.

| Record | What to capture |
|---|---|
| Event and observation window | Event type, start/end times, counted entrances, exclusions and missing intervals |
| Arrivals | Counts by 1–5 minute interval, measurement method and source |
| Service times | Anonymous durations for each service, sample size, start/end definition and unusual cases |
| Resources | Actual staff skills, posts, shift changes, handovers and equipment interruptions |
| Operating rules | Batch release, queue capacity, routing conditions, closing rules and the meaning of leaving early |
| Observed results | Completions, departures, unfinished visitors, queue lengths and waiting samples at the same cutoff |
| Original decision | What the organizer would do before seeing the tool, and why |
| Rehearsal finding | Which guest group, node, time interval or resource record changed their understanding |
| Revised arrangement | Exact parameter changes, extra staff/device hours, and rejected alternatives |
| Organizer trial | What they could complete unaided, where they got stuck, and whether they changed their decision |

Mark each parameter as measured, historical, estimated or synthetic, with its source. Preserve the original project and export the revised project and execution report. Compare the model's baseline with the observed event before presenting an alternative as useful; record discrepancies instead of tuning silently to one desired result. Use held-out intervals or another event when possible.

Report the simulated change separately from any observed later event. A single retrospective fit does not establish predictive accuracy, a causal effect, safety certification or widespread adoption. Record collection effort, incomplete data and the organizer's additional resource cost.

## 中文记录要点

当前尚未开展真实活动回溯、参数测量或组织者试用。选一场熟悉的校园活动，记录分时到达人数、各环节耗时样本、人员技能与岗位、设备中断和关闭/分流/放行规则。区分实测、历史、估计与合成来源。

保留组织者试用前的安排和理由，再记录工具让他发现的具体证据、最终是否改变安排、额外资源代价，以及不能独立完成的操作。先比较基线模拟与同口径的真实观测，公开偏差；将“模拟中的改善”和“后来活动实际发生的改善”分别报告。未收到这些材料前，不将本表填写成已完成验证。
